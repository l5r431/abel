Behind the scenes

Where the frames come from

Great photographs rarely happen by accident. They begin long before the shutter: scouting the light, waiting for the moment, and a lot of coffee.

Before your shoot

Four small things that make a big difference

A little planning goes a long way. These simple habits help every session feel easy and look natural.

Chase the soft light

The first and last hour of daylight flatter almost everyone. We’ll plan your shoot around it.

Wear what feels like you

Soft, solid colours photograph beautifully, and comfort always shows in the pictures.

Pick a place with meaning

A spot you already love makes it easier to relax, and the photos feel more like you.

Book early for busy seasons

Weddings fill 6–9 months ahead; portrait sessions are usually free within 2–3 weeks.

Questions

How far ahead should I book?

Weddings fill 6–9 months ahead; portrait sessions are usually available within 2–3 weeks.

When do I receive my photos?

A small preview within 48 hours, then the complete edited gallery within two weeks.

Do you travel?

Gladly. Travel is quoted per project and often makes for the best stories.

What should we wear?

Soft, solid colours that suit the setting work best. We’ll talk through ideas together before the shoot.
