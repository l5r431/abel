Questions

Good to know

How far ahead should I book?

Weddings fill 6–9 months ahead; portrait sessions are usually available within 2–3 weeks.

Do you travel?

Gladly. Travel is quoted per project and often makes for the best stories.

When do I receive my photos?

A small preview within 48 hours, then the complete edited gallery within two weeks.

Do you offer prints and albums?

Yes, fine-art prints, framed pieces and handbound albums, all made to order.

What should we wear?

Soft, solid colours that suit the setting work best. We’ll talk through ideas together before the shoot.

How does the deposit work?

A 30% deposit secures your date and the balance is due a week before the shoot. I’ll send a simple booking agreement to confirm everything.

What if it rains?

We’ll agree a backup date or an indoor alternative in advance. Soft, overcast light can be beautiful too.

Can I have the unedited files?

I deliver a fully edited gallery. Unedited files aren’t part of the standard package.

Can I use the photos commercially?

Personal use is included. For business or commercial use, I’ll provide a tailored licence.

Do you work with a second photographer?

For larger weddings, yes. It’s an optional add-on we can plan together.

Can children and pets join?

Always. They’re often the best part of the gallery, and I’m patient with both.
